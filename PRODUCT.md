# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Climate-curious listeners, practitioners, and partners across Africa and the diaspora who want serious conversations on climate finance, energy, circularity, entrepreneurship, policy, food systems, and African leadership. They arrive to find an episode, hear a guest, or browse by theme, often on phone, sometimes sharing a quote or episode page.

## Product Purpose

The public home of *Sustainably Speaking Africa*: a digital climate storytelling experience where sound, people, and African innovation are the main language. Success looks like a cinematic but usable homepage, topic-led discovery, shareable episode pages (SEO and social), and a clean searchable archive, without forcing autoplay or burying substance under effects.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, editorial, forward. Mood: African editorial magazine plus modern radio studio plus climate innovation laboratory. Confident, elegant, slightly dark; movement from challenge to solution.

## Anti-references

- Generic green corporate NGO or climate sites
- A greener clone of Inkomoko (use Inkomoko for information architecture only)
- Stock globe or map gimmicks that do not aid discovery
- Podcast sites that are only YouTube embeds and card grids
- Motion-heavy demos that feel unserious or block access

## Design Principles

1. **Sound, people, place** — Portraits, frequency and waveform language, and African climate stories carry identity; decoration does not.
2. **Editorial media, not brochure** — Oversized type, pinned storytelling, quote moments; credibility over template polish.
3. **Discovery before chronology** — Topics and voices lead; the archive stays fast and filterable underneath.
4. **Challenge to solution** — Narrative arc of African agency (building, speaking, leading), not deficit tourism.
5. **Motion with restraint** — Animation serves story and continuity; transform and opacity, reduced-motion paths, mobile simplification.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG-minded contrast; no forced autoplay; keyboard-accessible player; full transcripts; reduced-motion alternatives; compressed portraits; lazy embeds; usable static fallbacks when JavaScript or heavy scroll sequences are not available.
